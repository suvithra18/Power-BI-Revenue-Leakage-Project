# 💰 Revenue Leakage & Financial Risk Analytics

### Financial Intelligence Dashboard for Revenue Protection & Risk Monitoring

## 📌 Project Overview

Revenue Leakage & Financial Risk Analytics is an end-to-end financial intelligence solution developed using **Power BI, SQL, and Python**. The project helps organizations identify, measure, and prevent revenue losses caused by delayed payments, partial collections, SLA penalties, and billing inefficiencies.

The dashboard provides finance teams and business leaders with actionable insights to improve cash flow visibility, reduce financial risks, optimize collections, and support strategic decision-making.
